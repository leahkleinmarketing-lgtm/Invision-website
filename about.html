Our story

Beautiful hair starts with you.

In Vision Hair Design was founded on the belief that great hair starts with understanding the person in the chair. A visit here is a conversation about how you want to look and feel, followed by a thoughtful approach to getting there.

Our team combines technical skill with a warm, welcoming experience, whether you’re maintaining the style you love or ready for something new.

What that means for your hair

Personal care, whatever brings you in.

Our services cover the everyday refresh and the bigger transformation. The common thread is a plan shaped around you.

Color with intention

A root touch-up can keep a favorite shade fresh. Custom color and highlights can add dimension or mark a change. We talk through your goals and upkeep before choosing the approach.

A cut that fits your life

Women’s and men’s haircuts are designed for your texture, style, and routine. Blowouts and styling help you enjoy the finished look, while perms offer another way to change its shape.

More possibilities, with care

Hair extensions can add length or fullness. Treatments and professional products support the health and feel of your hair as we work toward the result you want.

For every client

A look that feels like yours.

Some clients come in for a dependable root refresh or a precise haircut. Others are exploring a new color, added length, a perm, or a polished blowout. We start in the same place: listening.

That conversation helps us recommend services that suit your hair and the way you live with it.
