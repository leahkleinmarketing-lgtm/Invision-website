Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 9, 2026

In Vision Hair Design (“we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit https://www.invisionhairdesign.com/ (the “Site”) or contact us.

1) Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Information you provide to us

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number
  • Messages and details you submit through our contact forms or inquiries
  • Appointment-related details you choose to share (for example, service preferences)

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and approximate location (city/state)
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, and time spent on the Site
  • Referring/exit pages and general usage data

Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to help the Site function, understand traffic, and improve performance.

2) How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and communicate with you
  • Provide and improve our services and customer experience
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the Site
  • Understand how visitors use the Site (analytics)
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies

3) How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Site and business (for example, website hosting, forms, analytics, invoicing/payment processing)
  • If required by law (such as responding to lawful requests)
  • To protect rights and safety (to prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues)
  • With your consent or at your direction

4) Payments

If you make a payment through the Site, payment information is processed by our payment processors. We do not store full payment card details ourselves. Your payment is subject to the processor’s privacy practices in addition to this policy.

5) Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

6) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7) Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of certain communications (for example, marketing emails, if applicable)

To make a request, contact us using the information below.

8) Children’s Privacy

Our Site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9) Third-Party Links

Our Site may contain links to third-party websites or services (for example, social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.

10) Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
