Welcome to In Vision Hair Design. These Terms and Conditions apply to your use of our website and, where applicable, appointments and salon services offered by In Vision Hair Design (“we,” “us,” or “our”). Please read them before scheduling a visit. Nothing in these Terms restricts rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

1. Services and Consultations

We provide haircuts, color, styling, and related salon services. Appointments, service descriptions, estimated durations, and advertised prices may change.

Your stylist may recommend modifying or declining a service where hair condition, prior chemical treatment, allergies, sensitivities, or another safety concern makes the requested service unsuitable.

Please tell us about relevant hair history and sensitivities before a service. Results may vary based on hair condition and previous treatments.

2. Booking and Arrival

Appointments can be made through our booking system, by phone, or directly with the salon.

Please arrive on time. If you arrive late, we may shorten, modify, or reschedule your appointment to protect the next guest’s scheduled time. We will explain material changes where practical.

3. Cancellation and No-Show Policy

We kindly request at least 24 hours’ notice when canceling or rescheduling an appointment.

  • Appointments canceled or rescheduled within 24 hours of the scheduled start time will incur a fee equal to 30% of the scheduled service cost.
  • Guests who do not attend their appointment without notifying the salon will incur a 30% no-show fee.

Fees apply only to the extent allowed by law and as disclosed and authorized during booking.

We understand that unexpected situations may arise. Please contact us promptly if exceptional circumstances require consideration.

4. Service Pricing and Additional Charges

All service prices displayed on our website are starting prices unless otherwise specified.

Final pricing may vary depending on:

  • Hair length and density
  • Hair condition and previous chemical treatments
  • Amount of product required
  • Time and complexity of the service
  • Additional services requested during consultation

Additional color, long-hair work, corrective services, extra product, or blow-drying may involve separate charges when not included in the original booking.

We will communicate material additional charges before proceeding with the additional work.

5. Satisfaction, Refunds and Service Adjustments

At In Vision Hair Design, your satisfaction is our priority.

All completed salon services are non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

If you have any concerns about your hair service, please contact us within 7 calendar days of your original appointment.

We offer one complimentary service adjustment, when appropriate, to address concerns directly related to the original service.

This policy applies to all hair services, including haircuts, hair coloring, highlights, balayage, and styling.

Complimentary adjustments are intended to refine the original service, not to provide a different hairstyle, color, or additional service.

Changes in personal preference, requests for a new look, or adjustments requested after the 7-day period may require an additional charge.

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under applicable law.

Payment is due upon completion of services unless different terms have been communicated in advance.

Promotions and special offers may be subject to eligibility requirements, expiration dates, and restrictions on combining discounts.

Special promotional certificates may have separate lawful terms disclosed with the offer.
